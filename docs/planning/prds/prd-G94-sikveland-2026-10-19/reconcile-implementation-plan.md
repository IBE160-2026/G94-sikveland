# Reconciliation: implementation-plan.md vs PRD

- **Input:** `docs/planning/implementation-plan.md` (upstream working plan, predates the PRD)
- **Compared against:** `prd.md` (updated 2026-11-02), `addendum.md`, `.memlog.md`
- **Date:** 2026-11-02
- **Severity scale:** High = changes behaviour, scope or a tested requirement; Medium = a gap a story or test would hit; Low = wording, traceability or nice-to-have.

The plan says of itself: "the BMAD documents (PRD, architecture, epics and stories) will replace the details here as they are written." Where the plan and PRD disagree (group c), the PRD decision stands and the plan is the document to update later.
