Schengen visitor visa (C visa) for short stay

## **How do I apply for a Schengen visitor visa (C-visa)?**

A C-visa allows you to stay in the Schengen area for **up to 90 days within a 180-day period**. The same rules apply in all Schengen countries.

In Norway the application process is divided into two main parts:

* **Part 1: Online application and payment**
  First, you or the person who intends to travel to Norway must complete [an electronic application for a visitor visa on the UDI website](https://www.udi.no/en/want-to-apply/visit-and-holiday/).

Once the application form has been completed, the application fee must be paid online. The person who submits the electronic application will then receive a confirmation by email, along with a document titled **“Schengen.pdf”**. This document must be printed and signed personally by the applicant.

* **Part 2: Submit documents in person**
  After the application has been registered online and the fee has been paid, you must book an appointment with a VFS Global [Visa Application Center](https://www.vfsglobal.com/en/individuals/index.html) to submit your application and the supporting documents.

As an applicant for a visitor visa, you are generally required to appear in person at a visa application center in your home country or country of residence to submit the application and all required documents (see below for exemptions).

The required documents are listed in the relevant [**checklist**](https://www.udi.no/en/checklists-container/how-to-apply-for-a-visitors-visa/?r=tha).

In most cases, you must visit a visa application center in person. However, if you have applied for a Schengen visa within the last five years (59 months) and your biometric data is still valid, you may not be required to appear in person. In such cases, a representative may submit your visa application documents for you. To do so, they must have a signed [letter of authorization (power of attorney)](https://www.udi.no/en/word-definitions/power-of-attorney--authorisation-/) from you. Please note that this option applies to **visa applications only**. The embassy or consulate may still require you to appear in person if deemed necessary.

You can find more information on C-visas **[here](https://www.udi.no/en/answer-pages/frequently-asked-questions-about-visitor-visa/)**.

Entry visa (D visa)

## **What is the difference between C visas and D visas?**

A C visa allows short stays of up to 90 days and is processed under a different set of rules than D visas. Also, the standard processing time of up to **15 days applies only to C visas**.

By contrast, a D visa is subject to separate national regulations, and its processing time may be significantly longer. Norway issues D visas mainly as entry visas for people who have recently been granted a residence permit or who have lost their residence card.

## **How do I apply for an entry visa (D visa)?**

Please note that UDI implemented a new application process for D visas on 13 January 2026.

Information on how to apply for a D visa is available on UDI’s website [**here**](https://www.udi.no/en/word-definitions/entry-visas-d-visas/#link-38884).

## **Can I travel to Norway before my application has been processed by applying for an entry visa (D visa)?**

Only in rare cases. To be considered, it must be beyond doubt that the applicant will be granted a residence permit for family immigration. This means that all requirements must be fully met and that the application is complete, clear, and does not require further assessment.

In practice, it is often difficult for the Embassy to determine in advance whether a family immigration application will be approved. The Embassy must also consider whether UDI is likely to request additional information or require interviews in the specific type of case. For these reasons, **most applications for entry visas are refused**, and D visas are granted only in rare cases.

You can find more information on D visas [here](https://www.udi.no/en/answer-pages/answers-family-immigration/#link-36547).

Business Travellers (short stay)

## **How do I apply for a short-stay business visa (up to 90 days)?**

You must follow the same application procedure as for other visa purposes. For detailed information on the application process, please refer to the **“Schengen Visitor Visa (C-visa)”** tab.

## **Can I travel to Norway on a short-stay C visa to work?**

It depends on the activities you will carry out in Norway. As a general rule, you need a work permit to work in Norway, unless your activities fall under an applicable exemption. See the relevant guidelines: **[UDI 2011-032](https://www.udiregelverk.no/en/documents/udi-guidelines/udi-2011-032/)**.

It is therefore important that both you and your inviting party in Norway check the applicable rules in advance and provide sufficient information about the activities you intend to do during your stay in Norway.

## **Can the embassy prioritise my visa application if I need my visa urgently?**

Business visa applications are generally given priority and processed as quickly as possible.

To help the embassy assess your application promptly, ensure that you provide clear and sufficient documentation from both your employer in your home country and the inviting company or organisation in Norway. The documentation should clearly explain the purpose of your visit and the activities you intend to carry out during your stay in Norway.

## **Do I need to appear in person to submit my application at VFS Global?**

In most cases, you must submit your application in person at a VFS Application Centre. However, if you have previously applied for a Schengen visa within the last five years (59 months) and your biometric data is still valid, you may not need to appear in person. In such cases, a representative may submit the application on your behalf.

The representative must have a signed [authorization letter (power of attorney)](https://www.udi.no/en/word-definitions/power-of-attorney--authorisation-/) from you.

Photo requirements - all applications

## What are the requirements for the photo submitted with the application?

### The photograph must meet the following requirements:

* Be in sharp focus, clear, and with good contrast
* Be printed on high-quality photo paper and in high resolution
* Be in color with a white background
* Measure 35–40 mm in width
* Be no older than 3 months and clearly resemble the applicant
* Show a full-face view with the applicant facing the camera directly and looking straight into the camera
* Show the entire head and the top of the shoulders, with the face occupying 70–80% of the photo
* Show the eyes clearly and with the eyes open; hair must not cover the eyes
* Show a neutral facial expression with the mouth closed (teeth should not be visible)
* Not contain glasses or visible marks such as glue or staples

**Head coverings:**
Religious head coverings are permitted provided that all facial features are clearly visible.

* The chin, forehead, and both cheeks must be fully visible
* No shadows may cover the face due to the head covering

Application submission and appointments

## **Where do I submit my application?**

All visa and residence permit applications must be submitted in person at a [**VFS Global Norwegian application center**](https://www.vfsglobal.com/en/individuals/index.html) in your home country or country of residence. In Schengen visa cases, some exemptions apply (check FAQ: *How do I apply for a Schengen visa (C visa)?*)

Please note that applications may be lodged in Thailand only by citizens of Thailand, Cambodia, or Laos, or by individuals legally resident in Thailand.

## **What should I do if I have difficulties booking an appointment?**

If you experience difficulties booking an appointment, please contact the **VFS Global call center** directly for assistance.

## **How early should I apply for a Schengen visa (C-visa)?**

To allow sufficient processing time, we recommend submitting your Schengen visa application **at least 4 weeks before departure**.

* You may apply **up to 6 months** before your planned travel date
* Seafarers may apply **up to 9 months** in advance
* Applications must be submitted **no later than 15 calendar days** before travel

Waiting time and priority processing

## **How long do I have to wait for an answer?**

Processing times are calculated from the date the application or appeal is received at the Embassy.

### **Schengen visa (C visa)**

* Up to **15 calendar days**
* In some cases, processing may take up to **45 calendar days** if further examination is required

### **Appeals (C visa)**

* Up to **six (6) months** waiting time
* Processing may take longer if the appeal is forwarded to UDI

### **Entry visa (D visa)**

* **Lost or expired residence card:** approximately **6 weeks** waiting time. However, if the embassy needs to verify your permit with the Norwegian Directorate of Immigration (UDI), the processing time may be **extended by several months**.
* **Family immigration:** approximately **six (6) months** waiting time

### **Residence permits**

All residence permit applications are processed by **UDI**. Estimated waiting times are available here: [**Waiting time – UDI**](https://www.udi.no/en/word-definitions/waitingtime/)

For questions regarding a **pending application or appeal at UDI**, please contact UDI directly: [**Contact us – UDI**](https://www.udi.no/en/contact-us/)

## **When can I request priority processing?**

The Embassy can only give priority to **well-documented urgent cases**.
Pre-paid travel arrangements are **not** considered a valid reason for urgency.

Residence permit granted

## My residence permit has been granted. How do I get my D-visa (entry visa) issued?

To have your D-Entry visa issued, you must submit the following documents to your local [VFS office](https://www.vfsglobal.com/en/individuals/index.html):

* Your **original passport**
* **One passport-sized photo** with a white background (taken within the last **six months** and not edited, airbrushed, or photoshopped)
* A **copy of the UDI notification of decision**

Once submitted, your passport will be forwarded to the **Royal Norwegian Embassy in Bangkok** for visa issuance. The process normally takes **approximately 7–10 days**. After the visa has been issued, your passport will be returned to your local VFS office, where you can collect it.

Changes and corrections

## **What should I do if there are spelling mistakes on my name, my middle name is missing, or my passport number is incorrect on my visa?**

For spelling errors or an incorrect passport number, please return your passport to your local VFS center so they can arrange the correction—no need to contact the Embassy directly. If your middle name is missing, this is not an issue; visas follow the information in the passport’s MRZ, which typically does not include middle names. As long as your first and last names are correct, the visa remains valid.

## **What should I do if my travel dates change after my visa has already been issued?**

Issued visas cannot be amended. Any change to travel dates – even by one day – requires submitting a new visa application, as updated information must undergo full reassessment. To avoid delays, please ensure all details, especially travel dates, are final before applying.

Lost or expired residence card

**I do not have my residence card. Can I still travel?**

We advise against travelling abroad without a valid residence card.

If you do not have a valid residence card but your residence permit in Norway is still valid, you must apply for a **D-visa** in order to travel back to Norway (see info below).

## **I am not sure if I have a valid residence permit in Norway. What should I do?**

If you are unsure whether your residence permit is still valid, please [**contact** **UDI**](https://www.udi.no/en/contact-us/).

Appeals

## **How do I appeal a decision from the Embassy?**

All appeals must be submitted through [**UDI’s document upload service**](https://www.udi.no/en/word-definitions/forwarding-documents/).

Please note:

* Appeals must be submitted **within 3 weeks** from the date you were informed of the decision
* The appeal must include your **handwritten signature**
* If someone submits the appeal on your behalf, they must have a [**valid power of attorney**](https://www.udi.no/en/word-definitions/power-of-attorney--authorisation-/)

### **Important Information:**

* Appeals sent by **email to the Embassy will not be registered or considered**
* If you appeal because your visa is valid for a shorter period than requested, the appeal will **not be considered if you use the visa**
* The Embassy is unable to provide individual guidance on refusals or appeal procedures

For more details, please visit:

[**Appealing a decision – UDI**](https://www.udi.no/en/word-definitions/appealing-a-decision/)

Cancellation, refunds and fees

## **How can I cancel my application and request a refund?**

The application fee can only be refunded if the application **has not been submitted at VFS Global**.

To request a cancellation and refund, please email the following documents in **PDF format** to: **visa.bangkok@mfa.no**

Required documents:

* UDI’s confirmation of paid fee from online registration
* A signed request letter for cancellation and refund (handwritten signature)
* A copy of your passport (biodata page)

Passport submission and return

## Can I get my passport back while my application is being processed?

**C visa applications:** Your passport must be submitted to VFS and will normally be kept by the embassy until your application has been processed. If you need your passport back before the application is finalized, you must withdraw your application. Please note that the application fee is non-refundable.

**Residence permit and D visa applications:** You may request the return of your passport while your application is being processed. If your application is approved, you will need to submit your passport to VFS again. Additional VFS fees may apply.

Using valid Schengen Visa

## Can I travel to Norway with a Schengen visa issued by another state?

Yes. A valid Schengen visa generally allows you to travel to Norway and other Schengen states during its period of validity. However, a visa does not automatically guarantee entry into Norway. You must also meet all entry requirements, including providing any required supporting documents and ensuring that your travel purpose matches the purpose stated in your visa application.

## Can I travel with a valid Schengen visa in my old passport?

Yes. If your Schengen visa is still valid, you do not need to transfer it to your new passport or apply for a new visa just because you have received a new passport.

When travelling, you must carry both:

* your **old passport**, containing the valid visa; and
* your **new valid passport**.

The visa remains valid until its expiry date, provided it has not been damaged, cancelled or revoked. You must present both passports when requested by immigration authorities.

## **Applying for protection (asylum)**

Under Norwegian law, you must be **present at the Norwegian border or inside Norway** to apply for protection.

If you are outside Norway, the responsible authority is the **UN High Commissioner for Refugees (UNHCR)**.
