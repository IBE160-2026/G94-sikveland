**Information regarding insurance policy**

Applicants are asked to show the original policy of their travel medical insurance and submit a copy of the policy with their application. Medical insurance is compulsory when travelling to the Schengen area on a visitor visa, and the Embassy is not able to issue a visa sticker until the insurance policy has been checked and registered in our data system.

The insurance must be valid for the whole period of the stay in the Schengen area, be valid for all countries in the Schengen area, and cover medical expenses of  minimum 30,000 Euro (or the equivalent in THB or other currencies), as well as repatriation.

[The following Thai companies](https://www.norway.no/contentassets/f0130372be264373abd19a30d9709485/thailand/list-of-travel-medical-insurance-companies-june-2020-tha.pdf) have signed a Code of Conduct. The choice of a particular insurance provider is up to the applicant.
