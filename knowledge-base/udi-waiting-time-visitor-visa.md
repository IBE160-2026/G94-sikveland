# Waiting time Visitor visa

Here you can find the waiting times for visitor visa applications. The waiting times are updated regularly and may change while you wait. We cannot say exactly when you will receive a decision, even if you contact us.

## Waiting times for visitor visa applications

Most applications are processed by the embassy, either in the country you live in or in another country. If the embassy is in doubt about whether you can be granted a visa, they will send your application to UDI, and you will be notified by email or SMS.

The waiting time is calculated from when you submit your application at an application centre, embassy or consulate. We cannot give more information about the waiting time, even if you contact us. Fewer enquiries can help everyone receive a decision faster.

| Application processed by | **Waiting time** |
| --- | --- |
| Embassy in your country of residence | 15 days |
| Embassy in another country | 15–29 days |
| Norwegian Directorate of Immigration (UDI) | 45 days |

## Waiting times may change

We usually process the oldest application first. Some applications take longer than others, so the waiting times are only a guide and cannot show exactly when you will get a decision.

### This can affect the waiting time

The waiting time can be affected by:

* The number of applicants
* Changes in your situation
* Conflicts and unrest in other countries
* Missing documents
* Additional investigations, such as [DNA tests and interviews](https://www.udi.no/en/word-definitions/additional-investigations-in-case-processing/)
* If you have a [criminal record](https://www.udi.no/en/word-definitions/convicted-persons/)

If anything is missing in your application, we will contact you.

## More information about waiting times

* [### Appeal after receiving a rejection](https://www.udi.no/en/waiting-time/visitor-visa/#link-38921)

  If you have appealed a rejection, you will receive a decision within

  6 months

   after you submitted your visitor visa application at the application centre, embassy, or consulate. In certain cases, it may take a little longer.

* [### What can I do to get a decision as quickly as possible?](https://www.udi.no/en/waiting-time/visitor-visa/#link-38920)

  You can make sure that:

  + Your application is complete and correctly filled out
  + All [necessary documents](https://www.udi.no/en/checklists-container/how-to-apply-for-a-visitors-visa/) are included
  + Your and your sponsor’s contact information is correct and up to date.
  + The person inviting you has [your authorisation](https://www.udi.no/en/word-definitions/power-of-attorney--authorisation-/) so that we can send important messages to them

* [### Can I request priority?](https://www.udi.no/en/waiting-time/visitor-visa/#link-38919)

  Very few applications can be prioritised. To request priority, you must send us

  + a detailed explanation
  + documentation, such as a medical certificate

  You cannot request priority by phone, chat or email.

  [See if you can be prioritised and how to request it](https://www.udi.no/en/word-definitions/can-your-case-moved-front-queue-priority/)
